# Procedural Generation Skill

## Overview
Generates algorithmic 3D objects, celestial bodies, and dynamic shaders matching realm aesthetics.

## Capabilities
- **Fibonacci Celestial Arrays**: Calculates star fields, Dyson collector spheres, and particle swarms.
- **Parametric Manifolds**: Generates non-Euclidean geometries, torus knots, and mobius strips.
- **GLSL Shader Generation**: Crafts fragment shaders for cosmic auroras, plasma cores, and bioluminescent ocean caustics.

## Guidelines
1. Always utilize instanced geometries (`InstancedMesh`) when duplicating identical primitives beyond 100 units.
2. Embed dispose handlers for all geometries and materials.
3. Keep uniform variables minimal to reduce GPU draw call overhead.
